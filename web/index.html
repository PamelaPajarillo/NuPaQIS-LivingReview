NuPaQIS: A Living Review of the Intersection of Nuclear and Particle Physics and Quantum Information Science

NuPaQIS is a collection of research at the intersection of nuclear and particle physics (NuPa) and quantum information science (QIS). As a living review, it is updated regularly to track new developments, and we welcome suggestions for papers to include.
